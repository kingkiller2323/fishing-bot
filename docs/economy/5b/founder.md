- **Public layer.** The catch card, its colour, the fish count, the time between cards and the rod's durability come from exactly the inputs a normal player's cast uses. The model runs `F.castOutcome` on the cast input unchanged, so the distribution is a normal player's by construction, not by tuning.
- **Private layer.** Each cast also makes the hidden reward rolls. Each roll is a draw from today's Founder rarity table (with the gear's rarity stats) and carries today's Founder pity. The fish are deposited privately. The private XP and sell multipliers apply to every base reward, public or private, and the account receives the product. `publicXp` receives only the public catch and base quest XP, so the public level is a normal player's.
- **Why rolls first.** Legendary+ and Lucky production (collection pace, M5) is the one target a multiplier cannot deliver. The rolls are sized for M5, and because each private fish also carries XP and value, they carry most of M1–M4 as well. The multipliers close the rest.
- **Durability.** The rod shows and loses exactly what a normal player's does, so its break points are public and normal. The advantage is paid as a private repair rebate instead of hidden durability. Hidden durability would move the public break point (Durability).
- **Gacha.** Today's Founder box luck is kept. `/open` replies ephemerally since hotfix L4, so box reveals are private. Buff slots are the exception (Remaining tells).
- **Gate.** The public level gates gameplay (`P-FOUNDER-GATE`). Under the real gate the Founder would fish above its public level, which is a tell of its own, so the hybrid is solved under the public gate only.

1. **The visible catch is capped, not hidden.** Founder bonus fish are added to the rod's normal multi-catch roll, and the total is capped at the normal maximum (`F.MULTI.maxFish`). That removes today's tens-of-fish cards. It does not make the cards look like a normal player's:

   Whether to accept these tells as the price of "not power", or to make the Founder look normal everywhere with all compensation private, is decision `P-FOUNDER-STEALTH` (§7.6).
2. **The Founder's power stays absurd.** The private XP and sell multipliers, Luck and durability efficiency are solved so every "preserved" metric meets or beats today (Targets). ### What shipped (`14e27f1`)

### 7.3 Durability

**The problem.** The engine charges `max(1, ceil(n × (1 − eff)))` per cast, so efficiency can never take a cast below one point. The Founder also casts faster. Under today's rule, **Founder rods would wear out faster per hour than a normal player's.**

## 8. Why these numbers (rationale)

- **Visible volume is cosmetic; the account is not.** Value and XP per cast are linear in the fish count, so the lost volume moves one-for-one into the private sell and XP multipliers. The public card never shows a multiplier: it shows `rewards.*.base` (`a2ab9a8`). It does show the rarities, the count and the cadence of the kept visible stats (§7.5).
- **Solved, not picked.** Each private value is the smallest that meets every target in §6 with headroom (`PARAMS.targets.margin`), rounded up (`PARAMS.rounding`). A framework change re-runs the solver; nothing is scaled by hand.
- **Kept values stay kept.** Rarity table, speed, bonus-draw distribution, pity, gacha stats, gacha pity and quest multipliers are read from `src/engine/balance.js` (`TODAY` in `founder.js`). The quests, streak and buffs designs already assume them.
- **Why the XP multiplier is large.** On crafted rods, the curve change raises the XP per level and the volume change cuts fish per cast. The proposed "absolute time to level" target (M2, `P-FOUNDER-TARGETS`, under the approved direction of decision 5) absorbs both, and the Founder is non-competitive.
- **Why the public gate.** A public card then never shows a biome or rod tier above the public level, and the real level becomes a private record, as decision 6 describes. The card's rarities, count and cadence still differ (§7.5). The Founder still reaches the world several times faster than a normal player (Public pace).

## 13. Migrations

Additive and idempotent (`P-FOUNDER-MIGRATION`).

- **`publicXp` (shipped).** Only accounts without the field are written, with a guarded update, so running it again is a no-op. `xp` and `level` are never touched.
- **`publicXp` follow-up (F4, small).** Two edge cases do not heal: a cast on a document without `publicXp` creates the field through `$inc` before the migration reaches it (the migration's `$exists` guard then skips it), and casts served by the old instance during a deploy overlap never reach `publicXp`. Minimal repair: write `publicXp` in the cast commit with an update pipeline (`$ifNull`), so a document without the field starts from its own `xp` at write time; reconcile once, setting `publicXp = xp` for every account whose applied journals carry no profile bonus and recomputing a Founder account from its journals (guarded, audited); and before the curve change, run a read-only production check that `publicXp = xp` for every non-Founder account. Production scale is small (Fix first F4).
- **`publicLevel` (proposed, at the curve change, with `P-CURVE-EXISTING`'s freeze), for every account.** Before the new curve applies, write `publicLevel` = today's-curve level of `publicXp`, only where missing; the level floor anchors on max(stored `level`, today's `levelForXp(xp)`). From then on the public level is `max(stored publicLevel, curve(publicXp))`, the same no-demotion rule as `level`, and every read and write site uses it (Level sites). Under the rescale alternative, `publicXp` is rescaled in the same idempotent step as `xp` instead.
- **The existing Founder account at the gate change (`P-FOUNDER-GATE`; not modelled).** The lifecycles start new accounts, so the one real Founder account's switch from the real to the public gate is not simulated. If its current biome (chosen under today's real-level gate) is above what its public level allows, its next `/fish` fails the gate (the world design's `BIOME_LOCKED` guard) and the failure shows on the public "No catch" card. Rule: before the public gate applies, move every `FOUNDER_IDS` account whose `currentBiome` is above its gate level to the highest biome its gate level allows (guarded on the old value, idempotent, audited). Its rod stats also drop once to the rods level cap of its public level; that is a one-time visible change of its catches.
- **Existing Founder fish** keep their stored `value` (today's sell era) and `valueBase`. The new sell multiplier applies to new catches only.
- **Profile values** are code (`balance.js`), not player data: a `BALANCE_VERSION` bump, with no document rewrite.
- **No catalog change** comes from this subsystem.

## 15. Risks

- **Remaining public tells** (Fix first; measured in §7.5). Under the proposal (`P-FOUNDER-STEALTH` option a) the Founder is identifiable, not merely lucky-looking:
  - the rarities printed on each card, and the card colour, identify it within a few cards on every tier (Detection);
  - on the Old Rod (below public Lv 20) most of its cards show more fish than a normal player can land (Visible catch);
  - cards can come faster than any normal reel allows when the Founder clicks quickly (Detection, cadence);
  - one `/stats`, `/collection` or `/inventory` reply shows the rarity histogram of every fish caught, and `/stats` also shows total fish caught, which grows several times faster than Normal's (One reply; Per tier, fish/h ratio);
  - rod durability shows a fraction of a point per fish where every normal rod loses exactly one (One reply);
  - `/open` reveals show the kept gacha luck (One reply);
  - F0, the money lines and the real-level gates are open (Fix first);
  - catch leaderboards exclude the Founder, so its absence can be noticed.

  The stealth option removes every card, durability, `/stats` and `/open` tell; only account-level tells remain (Stealth).
- **A live bug on the `/biome` and `/start-quest` menus** (F3): any member can act on the invoker's account. It predates Phase 5B and is not a Founder issue, but the proposed permit purchase would inherit it.
- **Day-30 money for the casual Founder** under the public gate (§2, Day 30).
- **The absurd sell multiplier makes Founder money meaningless next to Normal.** That is harmless while there is no trading. Any future trading or gifting must exclude the Founder profile.
- **Under the public gate, the real level has no gameplay effect.** It is a private record. The Founder still progresses several times faster than Normal (Public pace), but reaches Swamp after hours of play instead of minutes (Level gate).
