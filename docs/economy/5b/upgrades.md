# Phase 5B: Angler Upgrades (permanent cash upgrades)

**Status: analysis only.** Nothing here is live. Names, stats and prices are proposals (`P-UPGRADES-*`).

- **Framework 5b.5.** Every table is **generated** by `node scripts/economy/5b/render-docs.js` from `scripts/economy/5b/upgrades.js` `markdownTables()`; each block carries its provenance line. `check-shared.js` fails if a table is stale.
- **What it is.** Permanent, optional upgrades bought with cash, several increasingly expensive levels each, in the shop's **Upgrades** tab (`rods.md` §12). The goal is the Virtual Fisher feeling "I'm only $X away from my next upgrade" (`rods.md` §1) without a mandatory gate: every effect is small, nothing requires an upgrade level, and a player who never buys one still meets every approved window.
- **One model.** `upgrades` is a system on the shared lifecycle core, composed last in the reference loop (`integrate.js`), with its spend in the `optional` sink category.

- **Names** are proposals. **Casting Technique** shortens the cooldown (it adds to the rod's speed, within the engine's cap). **Fish Knowledge** is Rare Find. **Negotiation** is a sell bonus on fish caught (stamped at catch, like every sell stat). **Tackle Care** is durability efficiency (fewer repairs, under the stochastic durability rule `P-DURABILITY`). **Bait Conservation** is a chance per bait use not to consume the bait (the bait still applies). **Trophy Instinct** is trophy chance (more Giants). **Experience** is a small fishing-XP bonus.
- **No new currency, chest, charm or boat.** Upgrades are plain cash sinks on existing stats.

## 2. Purchase policies (`P-UPGRADES-REFERENCE-POLICY`)

| Policy | Rule | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **reference** | Buy the cheapest available level whose price is at most **1 h of the player's current income**, and only if the money left still covers **the next rod purchase and the next permit** (both within the next 10 levels). Rods and permits are always funded first (upgrades have the lowest purchase priority). | The reference loop (`F.UPGRADE_POLICY`), every archetype |
| **greedy** | Buy every available level the moment the money is there, **ahead of rods and permits**, no reserve. | The stress case for the permit proof |
| **none** | Never buy. | The "never upgrade" player: the optional-ness check |

**Why the reference player buys upgrades.** Your north-star loop includes "buy a standard rod **or permanent upgrade**", so the reference player does. The reserve means the policy cannot delay a rod or a permit by construction, and the windows are checked with it **and** with `none`, so the XP upgrade only helps the player who buys it and never becomes necessary.

## 8. Implementation notes (after approval; nothing changes now)
- **Data:** an additive `upgrades` sub-document on the user (`{ casting: 0, … }`; absent = 0). No backfill.
- **Purchase:** `buy-upgrade.js` (Upgrades tab): one select, the guarded click-time debit (C5 pattern) plus a conditional `$inc` on `upgrades.<key>` keyed by the expected current level, so a double click buys once.
- **Effects:** `resolveModifiers` adds the stats (the engine caps apply); Bait Conservation rolls once per bait use in `cast.js`; Negotiation is a `sellBonus` like any other, stamped at catch.
- **Founder:** upgrades apply to the Founder like any player (the private multipliers compose on top).
- **Tests:** prices equal `upgrades.priceTable()`; a level cannot be bought before its unlock level or twice; the stats reach the cast; Bait Conservation never consumes more than one bait per use.
