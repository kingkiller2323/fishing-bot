# Phase 5B · Daily streak, Streak Crate, and retiring Top.gg

**Status: proposal only.** Nothing here is live. No `src/` file, catalog row or player document changes until you approve.

- **Framework 5b.5.** Every table in this document is **generated**. `node scripts/economy/5b/render-docs.js` fills each block from `scripts/economy/5b/streak.js` `markdownTables()` at the current framework version, and each block ends with its provenance line (version and shared digest). Prose cites numbers only by pointing at a table. `check-shared.js` fails if a table is stale.
- **One model.** The streak is a *system* on the shared lifecycle core (`lifecycle.js`). `integrate.js` composes it with rods, world, quests and buffs into the reference loop, and every lifecycle figure here comes from that integrated model. `streak.integratedRun()` is that loop plus an observational recorder, and `recorderParity()` proves it identical to `integrate.run()` (validation table, §10). The module has no time-stepping loop of its own. Static figures (box contents, stage rates, attendance) come from the framework's pure functions.
- **Counting rule.** The streak books only the *non-buff* contents of its boxes: fish at sale value, parts at salvage, usable bait at pack price. The Double XP and Double Cash buffs in its boxes are valued once, by the buffs system, from the streak's `box` events. Streak boxes hold no Lucky Draw (user decision; `P-STREAK-ITEM-POOL`). Box values in §4–§6 therefore exclude buffs. The lifecycle table in §6 adds the cash value of the streak's buffs, and the value targets are checked on that total (`P-STREAK-TARGETS`).
- **Hand-set values:** only `PARAMS`. Every non-obvious choice is a proposed decision (§1), and `check-shared.js` checks each record against the value the model runs.
- **Finished-design inputs:** `rods.salvageValue()` and `rods.assembly()` (crate helpers, not a gear source), `bait.prices()`, `quests.templateTerms()` and the proposed Founder profile `founder.founderProfile()`.

## 3. How a streak day is earned

**The DCC day.**
- `dayIndex(ms)` is the shared `F.dayIndex` (framework decision `P-DAY`): the streak and the daily quest roll over at the same moment.
- The start hour is one shared constant. If analytics show it lands at a busy local hour for most players, it can move.

**The gate (`P-STREAK-GATE`).**
- Each **successful cast** (a cast that lands at least one fish) increments `streak.castsToday` for the current DCC day.
- The cast that reaches the gate credits the streak day inside the same atomic cast commit, and grants the day's box through the cast's idempotent grant list.
- The catch card shows one public line: *"🔥 Day N streak! Streak Crate added (/open)"*.
- Counting casts instead of fish makes the gate identical for every rod, every multi-catch roll and every profile. A Founder's bonus draws never reach it sooner.

**Why credit automatically instead of with a `/daily` claim?**
- The gate *is* the play, so a player who fished can never lose a streak by forgetting a command.
- The credit is written in the cast's journaled commit, so it is exactly-once by construction: guarded by `appliedOps`, and recovered with the cast.
- `/daily` becomes the place to *see* the streak (§11).

**Why decay by a cycle instead of resetting (`P-STREAK-DECAY`).**
- Once a streak is established, an uncovered miss keeps the chest phase: the next chest still comes a full cycle of claims after the last one. The miss costs that day's box and a cycle of badge progress.
- A player therefore never loses a long streak to one bad day, but consistency still matters: before the first chest, a miss with no token sends a young streak back to zero.
- A long run of missed days resets the streak (rule traces, §3).

**The two new pool options** (`P-STREAK-FISH-POOL`) are small, generic extensions to `gacha.js`:
- **`fish: 'highestUnlocked'`**
  - For each rarity, fish come from the opener's highest *accessible* biome (the same check `/biome` uses, so permits apply) that has a fish of that rarity.
  - The per-rarity fallback keeps the box sound when Mountain Stream (only a few Ultra fish today) goes live.
  - `true` keeps today's meaning (every catchable fish), so the legacy boxes are untouched.
- **`fishShare: s`**
  - Within a rolled rarity that has both fish and items, pick the fish kind with probability *s*, then pick within that kind (with `unique` and `featured` weights as today).
  - Without it, a single-biome fish pool would be swamped by items at some rarities, and value would jump between biomes.

**Why these choices.**

- **Bait grants one pack (`P-STREAK-BAIT-PACK`).** At the new prices a single unit is worth almost nothing. Packs for bait the player cannot use yet keep, and are valued at zero until usable. ## 10. Integration contract

`system(opts)` returns a fresh lifecycle system each call; all per-run state lives in `state.sys.streak`. `integrate.js` runs it in the reference loop.

| Hook | What it does |
|---|---|
| `onDayEnd` | Default `credit: 'dayEnd'`. If today's casts (`state.castsToday`) reached the play gate, it credits the streak day with `advanceStreak` and grants the box, valued at the level the day's last step started at. An attended day that ends below the gate counts as a miss. |
| `onCasts` | Only with `credit: 'gate'` (the engine's timing): the day is credited, and its box granted, on the step whose casts reach the gate. |
| `onMissedDay` | A day not attended counts as a miss. Misses are settled by the engine rule when the next day is credited (`applyGap`): grace tokens first, then decay per uncovered miss, then a reset after a long gap. A reset keeps the tokens. |
| `sessionDone` | True once today's casts reach the gate: the earliest point `F.MINIMUM_DAILY` may stop. |

- **Ledger:** cash source `'streak'` only, holding `boxContents()`. `baitValue: 'items'` leaves bait packs out of the cash. The streak writes no XP and has no purchases and no spend items.
- **Events:** every grant calls `emit('box', { name: 'Streak Crate' | 'Streak Chest', count: 1, level, source: 'streak', streakDay, profile })`. The buffs system values the buffs from these events; the streak never values them.
- **Founder:** when `state.profile === 'founder'` (set by the founder system), `boxContents(..., 'founder')` uses the proposed Founder profile. The gate is the same for every profile.
- **For other modules:** `boxEV()` keeps its name-based profiles (today's `balance.js` profiles, which `buffs.js` relies on) and also takes a profile object. `lifecycle()` returns the integrated run in the design-stage shape (`reached`, `at`, `snapshots`, `final`).

## 12. Schema and migrations (additive, idempotent)

**`UserSchema`, new optional fields.** Every one is `default: undefined`, so no document is written until the player's first successful cast after release:

```js
streak: {
	count: { type: Number, default: undefined },      // current streak (days)
	best: { type: Number, default: undefined },       // longest streak (badges derive from it)
	total: { type: Number, default: undefined },      // lifetime streak days
	lastDay: { type: Number, default: undefined },    // DCC day index of the last credited day
	grace: { type: Number, default: undefined },      // grace tokens (0..STREAK.grace.max)
	castsDay: { type: Number, default: undefined },   // day index castsToday belongs to
	castsToday: { type: Number, default: undefined }, // successful casts that day
},
```

**Migrations:**
- **Player data: none.** Missing fields read as `defaultState()` (no streak, the starting grace token). Everyone starts fresh at release; nothing is back-filled and nothing is rewritten.
- `stats.lastVoted` and `stats.totalVotes` are kept as they are.
- **Catalog:** the bootstrap seed inserts `Streak Crate` and `Streak Chest` by name. It is idempotent: an existing row is left alone.
- **No `*Data` collection is touched.**
- The optional Voter's Crate exclusion is a code definition change. Owned crates keep their documents.

## 14. Risks

- **Voters lose the vote reward.** Players used to Top.gg cash will notice. Mitigations: the daily Streak Crate and the Streak Chest, owned Voter's Crates stay, a transition notice on `/vote`, and the optional supporter flair. It fixes exploit #3 of Phase 5 and removes an external dependency.
- **Top.gg ranking may drop** without vote incentives. That does not affect the game; you already confirmed inviting players to the Discord is fine.
- **The live buff bugs (buffs design B1, B2)** must be fixed before buffs from any source mean anything. Until then the streak's buffs are worth nothing, and an engaged player's streak value falls by its Double Cash share (lifecycle table).
- **Headroom under the caps is small.** The regular cap (5%, counting buffs) and the streak XP cap (2%) both pass with little room (checks table). Removing the Lucky Draw raised the Double XP and Double Cash odds per open slightly (§6). A later change to the gear path, the curve or the Rare item pool can cross either cap. If one fails, lower the Double XP / Double Cash odds with a Gacha V2 `featured`/`exclude` change. Do not add cash.
- **Lucky Draws from other sources can still be spent on Streak Crates.** The buffs system spends surplus Lucky Draws (from the Daily Box and events) on Streak Crate opens. That value belongs to its source, not to the streak, and is small next to the assembly rebate (buffs design, §8).
- **Double Cash timing** (framework decision `P-DOUBLE-CASH`) sets the buff part of the streak's value for engaged players.
- **The day boundary** can fall at an awkward local hour. It is one shared constant (`P-DAY`), so it can be tuned from analytics.
- **Engine change in `gacha.js`** (two pool options). It is small and generic, but it touches the box engine; tests 6–7 cover it.
- **Bait packs arrive before they are usable.** The shop text says where and when each bait works. They are valued at zero until usable. For every bait except Strong Magnet the wait is enforced by biome access: a bait works only in biomes that open at or after its shop level (bait design, §14 legacy-level table). Strong Magnet works in every live biome, so a pack of it reaches players below its shop level; it waits only if the bait design's use-time level check ships (bait design §13). Today's `/equip` level check never blocks (bait design, fix-first).
- **Founder reveal on `/open`** (`P-STREAK-FOUNDER`). The reveal is public and rolled with the Founder gacha stats and pity, so a Founder shows Legendary/Lucky slots more often than a normal player (Founder reveal). Accept it, or pick one of the two alternatives in the decision.
- **Alt accounts** could farm streak boxes, but there is no trading, so nothing can reach a main account.
- **The value tapers** with the stage (§6). This is deliberate catch-up. If you want the streak to stay equally relevant for veterans, raise the Chest's guarantee instead of adding cash.
