## 3. Standard rod ladder (the new reference gear path)

A player who never crafts has a clear save-and-buy loop, with one rod every ten levels. Power comes from a mix of stats, never from big fish counts:
- modest multi-catch, always under the 1.80 ceiling;
- Rare Find, Luck and Trophy;
- speed and durability;
- biome access. The Trusty Rod is the first rod to catch strong fish.

## 4. Custom rods: the specialisation path (Rod Workshop)

At each level, a balanced custom set beats the standard rod of that level by a few percent net $/h at a higher, variable cost, or it specialises. The requirement rule changes to the level of the highest-rarity part, and crafting opens at Lv 10. That removes the inherited rule, where four Common parts meant Lv 20–40. **Approving this is also what releases L6B**: rod `/craft` and `/equip` enforcement would then use this rule.

## 12. Decisions (approved)

- **D1. Hybrid progression: approved.**
  - **What:** the standard ladder, custom rods as the specialisation path with the highest-part-rarity level rule, the seven Angler Upgrades with six levels each, and the shop layout (Rods | Bait | Upgrades | Supplies | Aquarium | Special, with a separate Rod Workshop).
  - **Releases L6B**, once one condition holds: the Lv 10 Common custom rod must be modelled as a sidegrade or specialisation against the Trusty Rod, with a regression check that it cannot leapfrog the ladder (§4).
- **D2. Event budget: kept.** The steady-state buff target excludes events. The separate event-inclusive guard (20% / 10% / 7% / 5%) passes for every archetype (§9.1).
- **D3. XP bait: approved as proposed.** The hard guard is a net economic sink, and no milestone may arrive more than 12% sooner than the same no-bait run. Every archetype passes (§9.3).
- **D4. Founder stealth-hybrid: approved at the recommended setting.**
  - **Setting:** 7 private rolls per cast, XP ×35, sell ×25, 40% private repair rebate.
  - **Public output:** rarity, fish count, cadence, durability and pity are identical to a normal player's.
  - **Private layer:** Founder pity applies to the private rolls; Founder gacha luck applies to non-buff slots only; private rolls never advance quest, streak or bait progress; private rewards are delivered only privately. The Founder stays non-competitive.
  - **Stealth closure:** `/sell` and `/boosters` become ephemeral for everyone. `/aquarium` and `/pet` stay public.

Next step: the implementation plan for the balance release (`docs/economy/PHASE5B_IMPLEMENTATION.md`). Nothing is merged to `main` until you approve each step.
