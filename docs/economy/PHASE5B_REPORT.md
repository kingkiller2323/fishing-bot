What changes for a player:

- **Pacing.** Hours to Lv 20 stay close to today's, even though the curve's XP at Lv 20 rises. The extra quest and streak XP is absorbed (§2). After Lv 20 each level costs more on a smooth quartic curve, and it replaces today's cliff (Lv 20→50 in under three hours of play). Every gear tier is bought during the stage before its level and equipped on arrival, so a better rod never blocks a level (§6).
- **Catch volume.** A crafted rod no longer lands up to 15 fish. The normal catch averages from 1.1 (a Common crafted rod) to 1.8 fish per cast. The reference tiers T1–T5 are 1.2–1.8, with occasional 3–5 fish jackpots (decision 2). A value model and XP per rarity carry the difference (§6.4).
- **Money.** Income comes from fishing, plus quest, streak and buff cash. It pays for repairs (small, predictable upkeep), one crate assembly per rod tier and one-time biome permits (§7). The optional spending:
  - XP bait and the aquarium lower savings.
  - Money bait is a gross sink but a small net source.
  - Display tanks are the aspirational sink.

  Players can save (decision 10), and in the reference loop they keep most of their income (§13, `P-SAVINGS`).
- **Daily play.** Top.gg voting is retired. A DCC-native streak (Streak Crate / Chest) and typed daily/weekly quests let a casual player keep pace per hour played (§5, §9, §10). A player who logs in only for the daily minimum never overtakes an engaged player (R2).
- **Founder.** Fewer visible fish, the same power through private multipliers, a public level from base XP, and every Founder catch non-competitive. The Founder is still identifiable unless you choose the stealth option (§14).

## 15. Fix-first list

Decision 7 separates bug and exploit fixes from balance tuning and puts them first. The list has four parts:
- **§15.0** — urgent live problems: hotfix candidates awaiting your approval.
- **§15.1** — already shipped.
- **Correctness fixes on today's numbers** — they need no balance value and can ship before the balance release.
- **Exploit closures that need a proposed value** — each names the decision that sets the value, and they ship with the balance release once it is approved.

The module tables below mark which kind each row is.

**Ordering rules:**
- **Buffs.** B3 can ship alone now. B1 + B2 must ship in the same deploy as the catch-time Double Cash stamp and the removal of cash buffs from every sale path. Otherwise every held Double Cash unit becomes a real hour of sale-time hoarding (decision 8).
